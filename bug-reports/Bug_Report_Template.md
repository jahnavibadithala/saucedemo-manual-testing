## Reporting Guidelines

- Report a defect only when actual behavior differs from expected behavior.
- Include clear, repeatable steps.
- Use screenshots when they help demonstrate the problem.
- Severity describes the impact of a defect.
- Priority describes how urgently it should be fixed.
- Do not mark a defect as fixed or closed without appropriate verification.
